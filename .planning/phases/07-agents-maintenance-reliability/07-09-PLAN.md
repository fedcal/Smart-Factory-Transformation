Ship the fourth and final business-logic agent of the maintenance cluster: DowntimeAnalyzer. Unlike PM/RCA/Coach which are reactive (event-driven or operator-initiated), DowntimeAnalyzer has both reactive (NATS consumer ingesting live downtime events from 07-05 sim-textile generator) and on-demand (REST endpoint returning OEEReport + Pareto) modes.
