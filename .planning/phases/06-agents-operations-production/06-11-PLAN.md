## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-V6-multi-instance | Tampering | duplicate cron firing | mitigate | Helm replicas=1 + max_instances=1 + coalesce=True (Pitfall §5). |
| T-V6-missed-fire | Denial of Service | container restart loses window | mitigate | misfire_grace_time=300s catches up after restart. |
| T-V6-throttle | Denial of Service | scheduler-driven AnomalyDetector storm | mitigate | RateLimiter 12/h on detector side (06-02); scheduler retries respect coalesce. |
| T-V6-injection | Tampering | trigger payload | mitigate | Body Pydantic-validated at api-gateway endpoint (06-12); scheduler payload is constant. |
| T-V6-secret | Information Disclosure | gateway URL leak | accept | API_GATEWAY_URL is internal hostname, no secret. |
</threat_model>
