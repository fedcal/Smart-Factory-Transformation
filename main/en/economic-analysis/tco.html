    TCO — Total Cost of Ownership (3 Years)¶

    The three-year TCO covers the six operational and investment cost components for deploying Smart Factory Transformation at a single Mantis site (ECO-03/ECO-06). All values derive from params.toml via the reproducible script.

    6-Component Breakdown¶

    Component Description Annual (EUR) 3-Year TCO (EUR)
    gpu_amortization GPU/Server amortization (3 years) 5,000.00 15,000.00
    energy Energy (kWh x 0.25 EUR/kWh, continuous inference) 2,190.00 6,570.00
    fte_partial Partial FTE (ops + IT support, 1 FTE equivalent) 45,000.00 135,000.00
    change_management Change management (organisational training, 2 years) 6,000.00 18,000.00
    it_ot_integration IT/OT integration (OPC-UA bridge, NATS, ERP connectors) 3,000.00 9,000.00
    training Specific training (HITL operators and technicians) 2,000.00 6,000.00
    total 3-year total TCO 63,190.00 189,570.00

    Source: tco_table.csv — generated deterministically by tco_oepv.py.

    Calculation Parameters¶

    Parameter Value Component
    gpu_cost_eur 15,000 EUR gpu_amortization
    gpu_amort_years 3 years gpu_amortization
    energy_kwh_annual 8,760 kWh/year energy
    energy_eur_kwh 0.25 EUR/kWh energy
    fte_annual_cost_eur 45,000 EUR/year fte_partial
    fte_partial 1.0 (1 FTE equivalent) fte_partial
    change_mgmt_eur 12,000 EUR total change_management
    change_mgmt_years 2 years change_management
    integration_eur 9,000 EUR total it_ot_integration
    integration_years 3 years it_ot_integration
    training_eur 6,000 EUR total training
    training_years 3 years training

    Component Notes (ECO-06)¶

    1. GPU/Server amortization¶

    Dedicated GPU server for self-hosted inference (Qwen2.5-7B via Ollama/vLLM). Linear amortization over 3 years: 15,000 EUR / 3 = 5,000 EUR/year.

    2. Energy¶

    Estimated consumption ~1 kW average in mixed-mode inference (GPU active ~60% of the time). 8,760 h/year x 1 kW x 0.25 EUR/kWh = 2,190 EUR/year. The 0.25 EUR/kWh tariff is the ARERA industrial parameter (configurable).

    3. Partial FTE¶

    1 FTE equivalent (0.5 FTE operations + 0.5 FTE IT support). Average FTE IT/OT cost in Northern Italy textile sector including charges: 45,000 EUR/year. Dominant component: 71% of total TCO — key area for evaluation.

    4. Change management¶

    Organisational training, communication and change management costs (process, not technology). Spread over 2 years: 12,000 / 2 = 6,000 EUR/year.

    5. IT/OT integration¶

    OPC-UA bridge configuration, NATS JetStream, ERP stub connectors (MVP phase). Spread over 3 years: 9,000 / 3 = 3,000 EUR/year.

    6. Specific training¶

    HITL system usage workshops, operator and technician training. Spread over 3 years: 6,000 / 3 = 2,000 EUR/year.

    Calculation Formula¶

    gpu_annual         = gpu_cost_eur / gpu_amort_years
energy_annual      = energy_kwh_annual * energy_eur_kwh
fte_annual         = fte_annual_cost_eur * fte_partial
change_annual      = change_mgmt_eur / change_mgmt_years
integration_annual = integration_eur / integration_years
training_annual    = training_eur / training_years

tco_annual = sum of 6 components
tco_3yr    = tco_annual * 3

    Relationship with OEPV Model¶

    The TCO is the technical-economic component of the project. The discount on the offer (derived from the Base d'Asta of 108,000 EUR) is evaluated in the OEPV model separately. See OEPV for the tender score simulation.
